# My Favorite Song: One Last Time

* **Artist:** Ariana Grande
* **Genre:** Pop / EDM / Synth-pop
* **Album:** My Everything (2014)

## About the Song
"One Last Time" is an emotional, upbeat pop track that blends a driving electronic beat with a bittersweet, nostalgic message. It tells the story of wanting just one final moment with a loved one to make things right before parting ways.

> "So one last time
> I need to be the one who takes you home
> One last time
> I promise after that, I'll let you go"
> — Ariana Grande

## Why I Love It
The contrast between the energetic, danceable production and the melancholic, heartfelt lyrics makes it an unforgettable song that hits on a deeply emotional level.
